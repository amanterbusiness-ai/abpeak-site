What happens to my photo?

Your face is removed right on your iPhone, only your torso is analysed. The photo is not stored during the analysis, and your scans stay on your iPhone. All details are in the privacy policy.

Do I need an account?

No. No name, no email, no password. Open the app and get started.

How does a home ab workout with AbPeak work?

A mat is enough. If you have a resistance band, a dumbbell, a pull-up bar or a gym, AbPeak builds them into your plan. You choose 5, 10, 20 or 30 minutes per session or let your coach decide. The six-pack workout plan runs 12 weeks in three blocks.

Can AbPeak count calories from a photo?

Yes, with Pro. You take a photo of your meal, AbPeak suggests foods, calories and nutrition values, and you adjust them before saving. The photo is sent only for the analysis and is not stored. Your target sits at most 25 % below your daily need, never below your basal metabolic rate and never below a safe minimum. Under 18 there is no deficit.

How do I cancel?

Any time in your iPhone settings under your Apple Account, Subscriptions. So that the subscription does not renew, cancel at the latest 24 hours before the term ends. Payment runs securely through Apple.

Does AbPeak connect to Apple Health?

Only if you want it. You connect Apple Health voluntarily in Settings and decide in the iOS prompt what the app may read and write. AbPeak reads your weight and active calories and writes your workouts, the active calories used and your weight when you change it in the app. The data stays on your device, the app does not use it for advertising and does not share it with third parties. Your profile weight goes to the analysis during a scan, like the other profile details, with your consent. All details are in the privacy policy.

Can I scan a friend?

Yes, with Pro and guest scans. Your friend confirms on your iPhone that they are at least 16 and gives consent themselves. The guest’s photo, details and result are not stored anywhere.

Is AbPeak available for Android?

At launch, AbPeak is available for iPhone.
