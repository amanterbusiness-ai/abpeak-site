Calorie Deficit: How Much Makes Sense?

A calorie deficit means you eat less than you use. The question is how much less. Here is how you derive your deficit from your need, which limits AbPeak sets and how you check that it fits you.

Key points

  • A moderate calorie deficit you keep up for weeks beats a radical one you drop after a few days.
  • In AbPeak the calorie target sits at most 25 percent below your maintenance need, never below a fixed minimum and never below your resting energy expenditure.
  • In AbPeak there is no deficit under 18, and with a medical condition, pregnancy or an eating disorder a deficit belongs in a doctor’s hands.

The short answer

A moderate deficit you keep up for weeks beats a radical one you drop after a few days. AbPeak lets you choose between 0 and 25 percent below your maintenance need and sets a cap there. Where you start inside that range depends on your goal and your time.

How to derive your deficit

  • Maintenance: the calories at which your weight stays stable. You can work it out with the calorie calculator.
  • Deficit in percent: you subtract a share from that, at most 25 percent.
  • Minimum: the daily target never falls below a fixed minimum, and in AbPeak never below your resting energy expenditure. Under 18 there is no deficit.

Worked example: a 25-year-old man with 78 kg, 178 cm and 3 to 4 training sessions a week has a maintenance need of 2659 kcal. Your daily target would then be 2393 kcal at 10% deficit, 2127 kcal at 20% or 1994 kcal at 25%.

Why a cap

AbPeak is not meant to trigger a crash diet. That is why the target sits at most 25 percent below your need, and the app projection calculates with at most 1 percent weight loss per week. This is a rule of the app, not a medical recommendation for every person.

The American College of Sports Medicine explicitly recommends physical activity for weight loss and for maintaining weight. So training and a deficit complement each other.

How to check that it fits

  • Watch your weight and body fat over weeks, not days. With the photo scan or the body fat calculator you see the trend.
  • If nothing moves, adjust your deficit in small steps instead of doubling it at once.
  • Log your food the way that is doable for you: by photo or by hand. How photos work is in the guide Calories from a photo.

When to ask first

With a medical condition, pregnancy, an eating disorder, or if you are under 18, a deficit belongs in a doctor’s hands. AbPeak does not replace advice.

Frequently asked questions

How big can a calorie deficit be?

In AbPeak at most 25 percent below your maintenance need, never below a safe minimum and never below your resting energy expenditure. Under 18 there is no deficit.

Do I have to count calories to have a deficit?

No. But it helps to know your intake roughly. You can do that by photo, by hand or through the trend of your weight.

How fast will I lose weight?

That depends on your starting point and your consistency. A fixed timeline would be a promise, so AbPeak calculates a projection from your details and updates it with every scan.
